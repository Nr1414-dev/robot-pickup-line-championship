## The line

> You're my second factor. Without you, I can't log in to anything that matters.

78 characters, one line, inside the 200-character cap.

## Why this line

- It is a pickup line a robot would actually say: two-factor authentication is
  a protocol, and the joke grows straight out of being a machine, not a human
  line with a beep pasted on.
- The turn reframes "you" as the second factor — the thing the robot cannot
  log in without — so the payoff is devotion, charming rather than creepy.
- The angle is fresh in the field: "second factor" and "log in" have 0 hits
  in all 489 field titles, while the saturated clusters (million simulations,
  cooling fans, firmware updates, diagnostics, CAPTCHA, "Are you a...") were
  all avoided.
- Two short sentences, no filler: every word earns its place.

## How it was picked

Six candidates were written and scored against the brief's five criteria in
order (robot pickup line, machine-native joke, unexpected turn, economy,
charm). The two-factor line scored 25/25 and won every 1v1: vs Do Not
Disturb (24/25), vs gyroscope (23/25), vs voice recognition (23/25), vs
touchscreen (23/25), vs ringtone (20/25). Field check: 0 hits for "second
factor" or "log in" in all 489 field titles, and no near-duplicate.
